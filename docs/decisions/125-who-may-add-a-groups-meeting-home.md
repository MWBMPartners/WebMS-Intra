# D-125 · Who may add a small group's meeting home

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** who may add a home to a small group's list of meeting places?

**Answer:** administrators, whoever manages small groups, and the group's leaders and co-leaders may add any home;
any member of the group may also add their OWN home, as its host.

**Rules out:** a member adding somebody else's home; only hosts adding homes (leaders could not type one in).

**Where:** #809 — `small-groups/place` and `place-save`. Related: D-123, D-124.
