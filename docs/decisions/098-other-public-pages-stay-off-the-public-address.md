# D-098 · The portal's other public pages do not answer on an organisation's public address in the first release

**Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** an organisation may give the portal a public address of its own, such as
events.example.org (D-095), and the public door answers there (#493 P4). The portal also has
other sign-in-free pages — a single event's page, the public calendar, the "suggest an event"
form. Should those also answer on the public address in this first release?

**Answer:** **not yet.** The public address shows only the door (its page, feeds and script); the
other public pages stay on the portal's own address. Each decides its organisation the older way,
which on a public address needs its own safety review. The owner, 9 November 2026, choosing the
recommended option.

**Rules out:** serving those pages on a public address in the first release.

**Where:** #493 P4 (a public address answers door requests only); the deferred work is #876.
Related: D-095.
