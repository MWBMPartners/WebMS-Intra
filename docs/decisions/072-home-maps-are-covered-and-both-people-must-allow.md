# D-072 · Maps of a member's home are covered, and both people must allow them

**Date:** 2026-11-06  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** the member directory shows a small map around where a member
lives, drawn from OpenStreetMap, an outside map service — so a viewer's browser
tells it roughly where that member lives (to about 110 metres) and who is
looking. Should these maps come under D-058 in the same piece of work as the
profile pictures, and when the map is of somebody else, whose choice counts?

**Answer:** **yes, in the same piece of work, and the map shows only if BOTH the
member it shows AND the person looking allow it.** Off by default: a member's
map appears only when that member has switched it on and the viewer has not
switched it off; otherwise the location is shown as text. The owner, 6 November
2026.

**Rules out:** leaving maps for later; letting only the shown member decide;
shipping maps on by default.

**Why:** it protects members who never chose to be on a map, and it is D-058's
"strictest wins" applied to the one service where the person shown and the
person looking are different people.

**Where:** #778. Builds on D-058; related D-073.
