# D-113 · A kept worker's name and number both stay, and the portal says so plainly

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** under D-016 a pastoral visit keeps the visitor's account NUMBER and, since #703, their
NAME. An erased person's account is emptied, so other records still holding just their number (an
expense claim, a rate-limit record) used to show "[Deleted User]"; but with a name kept against the
same number elsewhere, anyone who can read the database or a backup can re-identify them on every
record that kept their number. Three places told members the opposite. Keep the name but drop the
number, or keep both and say so?

**Answer:** **keep both, and say so.** The name and the number both stay on the records D-016, D-110 and
D-111 cover; every sentence that told members an emptied account stops a record naming anybody is
corrected to say that an erased worker can be re-identified on their other records through the records
they made. The owner, 10 November 2026, declining the recommended option (keep the name, drop the
number).

**Rules out:** dropping the account number from kept records; leaving the reassuring sentences as
they were.

**Where:** #703 — `web/_apps/help/admin.php`, the `tblExpenseClaims` and rate-limit reasons in
`personal-data-catalogue.php` (shown on My data and Request erasure), `docs/gdpr-erasure-policy.md`,
the privacy notice. Extends D-016.
