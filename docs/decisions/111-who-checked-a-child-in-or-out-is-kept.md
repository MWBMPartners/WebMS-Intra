# D-111 · Who checked a child in or out is kept when that helper is erased

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** children's check-in records which helper checked each child in and out
(`tblKidCheckins.checkedInByID` / `checkedOutByID`) — arguably a safeguarding record. When a helper
is erased, should their name stay on those records? Their numbers were being removed.

**Answer:** **keep their names**, saved on the record when it is made, as for pastoral visits. Who handed
a child over, and to whom, stays answerable after the helper leaves. The owner, 10 November 2026,
choosing the recommended option.

**Rules out:** removing the helper from check-in records on erasure.

**Where:** #703 — `GdprEraser`, `personal-data-catalogue.php`, the check-in save paths. Extends D-016.
