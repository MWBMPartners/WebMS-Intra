# D-197 · Approving a suggestion that was cancelled earlier is allowed, with a visible warning

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** on the moderation page, approving a waiting suggestion always publishes it, whatever status it had. If it was cancelled at some point (and so carries a cancellation history), should approving be refused, allowed with a warning, or left as it is (#1081)?

**Answer:** the owner chose allowed, with a visible warning. The moderator sees that the suggestion was cancelled earlier and that approving it makes it public, and must confirm before it is approved. The status is not otherwise changed.

**Rules out:** approving such a suggestion silently; refusing to approve it at all.

**Where:** #1081 (the moderation page and its approve handler). Related: D-169, D-196.

**Supersedes:** nothing.
