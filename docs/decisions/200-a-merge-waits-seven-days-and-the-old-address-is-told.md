# D-200 · A merge completes no sooner than 7 days after its notice, and the old address is told

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** to stop an administrator who reset somebody's password or changed their e-mail from later merging that person's accounts (#1064), an account is barred from agreeing if somebody other than its owner changed its password or e-mail in the 90 days before the request or at any time since. A patient administrator can still wait it out. What should the rule be?

**Answer:** the owner chose: keep the 90-day bar, add a 7-day wait, and tell the old address. A merge completes no sooner than 7 days after its start notice; either account can cancel it; the start notice goes also to the address the account held before any change made by somebody else. The refusal and the notice say how long the bar and the wait last, and that a new request is needed after a bar.

**Rules out:** a merge completing the same day it is asked for; a notice that goes only to an address somebody else may have changed; a bar of no stated length.

**Where:** #1064. Related: D-190, D-191, D-018.

**Supersedes:** nothing.
