# D-168 · When a host leaves a small group, their home stops being its meeting place automatically

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** A small group can meet at a member's home, named as its host (D-120). When that host leaves the group, what happens to their home as the meeting place?

**Answer:** Removed automatically: their home stops being the group's meeting place straight away, and the group's leaders are told to choose a new place.

**Rules out:** Keeping a former host's home as the meeting place until somebody removes it.

**Where:** #952 — `Portal\Core\SmallGroups`. Related: D-120, D-123, D-124, D-128, D-131.
