# D-032 · The giving-records ceiling is opt-in

**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** should the portal enforce a maximum retention period for a
donor's name and address?

**Answer:** it ships with NO ceiling and nothing changes until somebody sets
one. The owner's words: *"can have the option to enforce such a limit - but can
also choose not to. so customisable"*.

**Rules out:** a built-in ceiling. The figure is a STATUTORY retention period,
and forcing it down could make a customer destroy records their own law
requires. This is the one limit where capping is the dangerous direction.

**Where:** `GivingRetention::capInForce()`; #687.
