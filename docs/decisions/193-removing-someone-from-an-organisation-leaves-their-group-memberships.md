# D-193 · Removing somebody from an organisation does not end their small-group memberships

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** when somebody is removed from an organisation (Admin, Organisations, Users, Remove) they stay a member of its small groups and any home they listed stays visible to the group. Should removing them also end their small-group memberships there (#1080)?

**Answer:** the owner chose "No, leave them". Their group memberships and homes stay until a group leader removes them or the person leaves, as before. The privacy and offboarding pages must not claim otherwise.

**Rules out:** ending group memberships as a side effect of removing someone from an organisation.

**Where:** #1080 (closed with no code change); a switched-off account is the separate #1086. Related: D-168.

**Supersedes:** nothing.
