# D-123 · A small group may rotate the home it meets in

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** small groups often take turns meeting in different members' homes. Should a group have one meeting
place, or be able to meet in a different host's home from one meeting to the next?

**Answer:** a group may rotate. The owner, 11 November 2026: *"Small Groups could also rotate who's home they meet
in….so this should be supported also"*. Each meeting place belongs to its own host: under D-120 it is that host's
home, its map is shown only where that host has agreed, and under D-122 erasing that host empties that host's place
— and only theirs. One host's agreement or erasure never affects another host's place.

**Rules out:** a single meeting place per group as the only option; one host's agreement standing for another's
home; erasing one host removing every place the group has used.

**Where:** #809 — small groups, `Portal\Core\OutsideServices`, `GdprEraser` and the personal-data catalogue.
Related: D-120, D-122, D-072.
