# D-142 · A worker's data download lists the records that carry their name, without the other person's details

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** a member's "My data" download is their copy of what the portal holds about them. Since D-110 to D-113,
pastoral care records, DBS checks and children's check-ins keep the name of the worker who made them. Those records
are mostly about someone else (the person being cared for, or a child), and the download left them out. Should a
worker's own download include them in full, list them without details, or leave them out with a note?

**Answer:** a list, with no details. The download lists each record that carries the worker's name: its type (for
example a care visit, a DBS check, a child's check-in), its date, and that their name is on it. Nothing about the
other person — no child's or cared-for person's name, no notes.

**Rules out:** giving the worker the full records (another person's details); leaving the records out of the
download.

**Where:** #929 — the data download (`GdprEraser::inventory()` and the "My data" page) and the personal-data
catalogue. Related: D-110, D-111, D-112, D-113.
