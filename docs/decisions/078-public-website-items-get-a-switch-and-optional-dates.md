# D-078 · An item on the public website gets an on/off switch AND optional dates

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** should "show on our public website" (D-057) be one on/off switch per
item, or a pair of dates — show from, show until (#493)?

**Answer:** **both.** Each item has an on/off switch, and may also carry a "public from"
and a "public until" date. The owner, 7 November 2026 ("Both"), choosing neither of the
two offered options alone.

**How the two combine** (the reading this record takes, to be built so): an item is on
the public website only while its switch is ON **and** today is inside its dates, where an
empty date means "no limit on that side". The switch is the master control; the dates can
only narrow it, never put on the website an item whose switch is off. An event's own end
and a poster's own display dates still apply as well.

**Rules out:** a switch with no dates; dates with no switch.

**Where:** #493, packages P1 (events) and P2 (posters). Related: D-057.
