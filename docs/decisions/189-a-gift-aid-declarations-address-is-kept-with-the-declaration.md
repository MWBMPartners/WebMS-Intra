# D-189 · A Gift Aid declaration's network address is kept with the declaration until its keep-until date

**Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** a Gift Aid tax declaration records the network address it was made from, which the tax authority does
not require. When the member who made it asks to be erased, should the address be emptied at once while the
declaration is kept for tax purposes?

**Answer:** the owner chose "Keep it until the tax keep-until date". The address is treated as part of the
declaration: an erasure does not empty it, and it goes with the declaration at the organisation's keep-until
date. The privacy pages must say so truthfully, including for a member who is never erased.

**Rules out:** emptying the declaration's address on erasure; any sentence saying the address is cleared sooner
than the declaration.

**Where:** #1019 (`GdprEraser`, the personal-data catalogue entry for `tblGiftAidDeclaration`, both privacy pages).
Related: D-178, D-116.

**Supersedes:** nothing.
