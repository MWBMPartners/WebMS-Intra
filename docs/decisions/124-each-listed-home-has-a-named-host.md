# D-124 · Each listed meeting home has a named host, whose agreement and erasure count

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force  
**Supersedes in part:** D-120, D-122

**Question:** when a small group meets in different members' homes (D-123), a leader often types in other members'
addresses. Whose agreement decides whether a home is shown on an outside map, and whose erasure removes it — the
person who typed it in (D-120, D-122) or the member whose home it is?

**Answer:** each listed home names its HOST, a member chosen on the form. Only that member can agree to a map of it,
and erasing that member removes their home. A leader typing in a member's address does not decide for that member.

**Rules out:** the person who typed an address in deciding the map for somebody else's home; erasing the person who
typed it in removing a home that is not theirs; erasing the real host leaving their home in place.

**Where:** #809 — small groups (homes list, meeting places), `Portal\Core\OutsideServices`, `GdprEraser` and the
catalogue. D-120's "treated as the host's home" and D-122's "removed on erasure" stand; what changes is WHO the host
is: the named member, not whoever entered it.
