# D-212 · "Remove" on the organisation users page stays a deletion; no leaving date is recorded for it

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** (#934, package 2h) on a portal with several organisations, Admin, Organisations, Users, Remove deletes a person's membership outright, so there is nothing to hold a leaving date (D-211). Should Remove be changed to switch the membership off instead?

**Answer:** the owner chose: no. Remove stays as it is and an issue records the gap. For several-organisation portals the person's consent records for that organisation are kept until replaced or erased (the safe direction). Offboarding is the way a leaving is recorded.

**Rules out:** changing who can manage or re-add a removed person as a side effect of the consent clear-out.

**Where:** #934 / package 2h; the follow-up issue.

**Supersedes:** nothing.

**Related:** D-211.
