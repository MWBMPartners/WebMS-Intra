# D-167 · Whether an organisation issues tax receipts for gifts is its own setting

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** The privacy notice and help mention keeping "receipted" gifts. Do organisations issue tax receipts, or should the words come out?

**Answer:** Make it configurable: some organisations issue tax receipts for gifts and others do not, so each organisation sets whether it does, and the privacy notice, help and the giving-records keeping rule describe receipts only where the setting says the organisation issues them. In the owner's words: "Some organisations may issue tax receipts for gifts/donations while others may not".

**Rules out:** Wording that assumes every organisation issues tax receipts; removing receipts entirely.

**Where:** #939 — the giving settings, privacy notice and help. Related: D-032.
