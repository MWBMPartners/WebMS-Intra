# D-112 · A worker's name stays on a visit they made on their own care case

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** rarely, a care-team member is also the subject of a care case and logs a visit on it (or
records their own safeguarding check). If they are later erased, their case notes are emptied, but
under D-016 their name would stay on that visit as the visitor, pointing back at them on a case
otherwise anonymised. Should it be removed in that case?

**Answer:** **keep it anyway** — apply D-016 uniformly: the name stays on every visit and check the
person made, including on their own case. The owner, 10 November 2026, declining the recommended
option (remove it when the visitor is the case's subject).

**Rules out:** a special case removing the visitor's name when they are the person the case is about.

**Where:** #703 — `GdprEraser` (section 19 of `tools/gdpr-eraser-selftest.php` pins it). Extends D-016.
