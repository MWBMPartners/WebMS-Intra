# D-126 · Removing a meeting home deletes its address at once

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** when a home is removed from a small group's list, what happens to its address?

**Answer:** it is emptied straight away. Past meetings held there say "a place no longer recorded".

**Rules out:** keeping a removed home's address until its host is erased.

**Where:** #809 — `small-groups/place-save`. Related: D-123, D-124.
