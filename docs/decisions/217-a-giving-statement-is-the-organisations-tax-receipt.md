# D-217 · The yearly giving statement is the organisation's tax receipt, for the privacy notice wording

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** in force

**Question:** (#939, Q-2e-1) when an organisation ticks "we issue tax receipts", what does the privacy notice say, given the portal records tax declarations and giving statements and never a receipt?

**Answer:** the owner confirmed the recommended answer: the giving statement is the receipt. The built wording stays: "gifts covered by a tax declaration, or by a giving statement (this organisation's tax receipt)".

**Rules out:** claiming the portal keeps names for receipts issued outside it (that would be a new build).

**Where:** `GivingRetention::receiptedGiftWords()` and `keptGiftWords()`; #939.

**Supersedes:** nothing.

**Related:** D-167.
