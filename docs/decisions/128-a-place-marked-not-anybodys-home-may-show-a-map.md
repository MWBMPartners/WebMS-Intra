# D-128 · A meeting place marked "not anybody's home" may show a map without a host

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** in force  
**Supersedes in part:** D-120

**Question:** a small group's usual meeting place may be nobody's home — a church hall or a coffee shop. Under
D-120 and D-124 a place shows an outside map only if its named host agrees, and a hall has no host. Should a place
that is nobody's home be able to show a map?

**Answer:** yes, if whoever sets the place ticks "this is not anybody's home". The map is then shown without a host,
still subject to the installation's, the organisation's and each viewer's own map settings (D-058, D-073). The tick
says plainly what it means, because a home address ticked by mistake would be shown.

**Rules out:** an ungated map of a place nobody has marked; a host's home shown without that host's agreement.

**Where:** #809 — small groups and `Portal\Core\OutsideServices`. D-120's rule stands for every place that IS
somebody's home; this decision adds the marked exception. Related: D-124.
